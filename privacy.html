XEON™ Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

1. Information We Process

XEON™ may process Discord-related information required to provide its features, such as user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, and configuration data.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide moderation, security, utility, automation, configuration, and other requested bot functionality.

3. Data Storage

Some configuration or feature data may be stored to allow XEON™ to operate correctly across sessions.

4. Data Sharing

XEON™ does not intentionally sell personal information. Information may be processed by Discord or other services required for the operation of the application.

5. Data Removal

Users or server administrators may contact the XEON™ development team regarding applicable data deletion requests.

6. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when XEON™ changes its functionality or data practices.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the XEON™ development team.
